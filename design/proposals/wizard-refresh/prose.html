Three steps

Recipients, message, review. You can go back to any step before anything is sent.

  1. 1

    Recipients

    Upload a CSV with an email column, plus any columns you want in the message, like first_name or company. Up to 5,000 rows. Rows with an invalid or blank email are skipped and listed in the results.

  2. 2

    Message

    Write once, and click a column name to drop in a field like {{company}}. Attach files if you need to. The preview shows each person’s copy as you type.

  3. 3

    Review & send

    Step through every recipient’s email, then send from your Gmail. Messages go out one at a time, 250 ms apart, and any that fail can be retried.
