Wizard refresh proposal

Static mockups for review. They use the app’s own tokens, fonts and components, and work in light and dark (toggle top right).

  1. No title rows on the three steps. The stepper already names the step; a hidden heading stays for screen readers.
  2. Centred stepper, at most 760 px wide, on every step.
  3. Quieter bottom bar. No “Step 1 of 3”; a disabled Next explains why in a tooltip instead of a line of text.
  4. Polish with AI moves into the editor toolbar (recommended), or shrinks to one compact row.
  5. Review is one summary card with plainer confirm wording. Results drop the title row too: the numbers, a status line on failure and the download and new-batch actions all sit in the card header.
  6. App bar (chosen): the email and Sign out move into an avatar menu, with Help and the theme toggle left in the bar. Text pages get a sticky contents rail beside the article (recommended).
